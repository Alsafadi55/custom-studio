Service Terms

LAST UPDATED: 25 OCTOBER 2026

This page outlines matters that should be agreed in writing before a project begins. The actual terms for each engagement should be set out in a proposal or service agreement.

Scope and deliverables

The proposal should identify the included work, deliverables, milestones and exclusions. Out-of-scope requests may require a revised proposal.

Timeline and feedback

Timing depends on the agreed scope and receiving required content, assets, decisions and feedback. The proposal should explain review rounds and how additional changes affect timing and cost.

Fees and payment

Fees, currency, payment schedule, taxes (if applicable) and third-party expenses must be agreed in writing. This page does not establish prices or payment methods.

Cancellation

The proposal should explain how pauses or cancellation work and how completed work, committed costs and outstanding fees are handled.

Ownership and third-party tools

The proposal should define usage rights, source-file delivery, portfolio display permissions and any third-party licenses. Hosting, domains, subscriptions and ongoing maintenance are excluded unless explicitly listed.
