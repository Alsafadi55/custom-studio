This page describes the current website's intended information handling. Review it against your actual practices before relying on it as a legal policy.

Information you provide

The contact form runs in your browser and prepares a message from the name, selected service and project description you enter. It attempts to copy that message and opens the CUSTOM STUDIO Instagram profile so you can send it yourself. The form does not submit its fields to a CUSTOM STUDIO server.

Browser storage

The site may store your selected language in browser local storage. It does not intentionally include analytics or advertising-tracking scripts.

Third-party resources

Google Fonts, Instagram and GitHub may process technical data when their resources load or you follow their links. They operate under their own privacy policies, outside this website's control.

Retention and contact

CUSTOM STUDIO does not receive the form fields unless you choose to send them through an external service. Do not send sensitive personal information through the form. For privacy questions, use the studio Instagram profile.

Updates

Update this policy if website features or actual data practices change.
